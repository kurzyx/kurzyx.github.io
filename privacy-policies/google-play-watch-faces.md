# Privacy Policy for Wear OS Watch Faces

**Effective Date:** November 24, 2026

**Kurzyx** ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes watch faces for Wear OS smartwatches. This Privacy Policy applies to all Wear OS watch faces and associated companion apps published under our Google Play developer account.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We respect your privacy. **Our watch faces do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or user information.**

- **Local Data Display:** Our watch faces may display system and sensor data on your smartwatch screen—such as time, date, battery level, step count, heart rate, or weather information. This data is read locally on your device using standard Wear OS APIs and complications. It is never stored on external servers or sent to us.
- **No Third-Party Tracking:** Our apps do not use third-party advertising networks, analytics tools, or tracking SDKs.
- **No User Accounts:** You do not need to create an account or provide any personal details to use our watch faces.

## 2. Permissions
Any permissions requested by a watch face (such as access to body sensors for heart rate or activity data) are strictly used locally on your smartwatch to display that information on the watch face itself.

## 3. Children's Privacy
Because our watch faces do not collect any personal information from anyone, they do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

## 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
